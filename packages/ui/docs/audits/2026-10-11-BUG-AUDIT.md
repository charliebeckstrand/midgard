## Mechanisms

**R1 (B01-C01).** File: `hooks/use-panel-resize.ts`. Mechanism: the press is gated on the primary pointer (`if (!isPrimaryPress(event)) return`), but the window listeners `pointermove`, `pointerup`, and `pointercancel` read no `pointerId`. Trigger: one finger drags a drawer or sheet grip; a second finger moves or lifts. The edge jumps to the second finger, and its release commits the gesture. Documented intent: "Resizing an edge-docked panel by its far edge." Reach: drawer and sheet playgrounds (`docs/pages/components/drawer/playground.tsx`, `sheet/playground.tsx`). Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R2 (B01-C02).** File: `hooks/use-panel-resize.ts`. Mechanism: a key step takes the committed size (`const measured = size ?? panel.getBoundingClientRect()[axis]`) and passes it to `floorOf`, but `drawerFloor` subtracts the live body height (`return height - body.getBoundingClientRect().height`). A key step sets no `data-resizing`, so the height transition still eases and the body reads taller than at the committed size. Trigger: hold ArrowDown on the grip of `<DrawerPanel height="half" handle>` under motion-safe. The panel shrinks below its chrome and the footer leaves the screen. Documented intent: "The drag stops at the chrome that does not scroll: the handle, a header, and a footer. The grip is a window splitter, so the arrow keys resize too." Reach: drawer playground. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R3 (B01-C08).** File: `layouts/sidebar/sidebar.tsx`. Mechanism: the floating body closes on `onPointerLeave={() => setFloatingOpen(false)}` with no pointer-type check, and a touch pointer fires `pointerleave` when the finger lifts. Trigger: a touch tablet at `lg` with the sidebar set to offcanvas; tap the hot zone, then tap a control in the sheet that does not navigate. Documented intent: "a hover-revealed floating Sheet"; nothing scopes touch out. Reach: `apps/admin/app/(signed-in)/client.tsx` (`UserMenu`, `AppearanceSettings` in the sidebar). Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R4 (B01-C09).** File: `layouts/sidebar/sidebar.tsx`. Mechanism: the pointer buffer went to `document.body` through a raw `createPortal`, so its `density-*` offsets resolved against the root scope, while the sheet went through `Portal`, which carries the nested scope (`data-density={density ?? undefined}`). Trigger: `SidebarLayout` inside a `DensityProvider` that differs from the root; peek the sidebar. The buffer covers 4rem of a `loose` sheet on the `chrome` rung and catches presses, or leaves a gap that closes a `compact` one. Documented intent: "It starts at the far edge of the floating sheet at each step." Reach: `docs/pages/providers/density/playground.tsx`. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R5 (B01-C11).** File: `recipes/kiso/panel/grip.ts`. Mechanism: `const BAR = ['rounded-full', ...mode('bg-zinc-950/20', 'dark:bg-white/25'), ...FOCUS]` paints only a background, which forced colors replace. Trigger: a drawer or sheet with `handle` under `forced-colors: active`. The grip is invisible at rest. Documented intent: "the bar inside it the reader actually sees". Reach: drawer and sheet playgrounds. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R6 (B01-C14).** File: `components/drawer/drawer.tsx`. Mechanism: `half: ['h-1/2 rounded-t-xl', ...]` keeps its corners whatever the dragged height, and `drawerCeiling` lets a drag reach the full screen. Only `fit` stamps `data-full`. Trigger: drag the grip of `<DrawerPanel height="half" handle>` to the top. Documented intent: "`full` squares the top corners, because a rounded corner against the screen edge reads as a panel that failed to reach it." Reach: drawer playground. Severity: medium (raised in the overturn pass: the result is visible and lasts until the drawer closes). Prior art: NONE.

**R7 (B02-C01).** File: `components/menu/use-menu-state.ts`. Mechanism: the in-panel guard `if (refs.floating.current?.contains(event.target as Node)) return` tests only the root panel. A submenu panel portals on its own, but its `contextmenu` bubbles through React to the root's `onContextMenu`, which calls `openAt` and anchors the root on an element in its own submenu. Trigger: right-click or long-press a row in an open submenu of a context menu. Documented intent: "A right-click inside the open panel must not re-anchor or reposition it". Reach: grid column menu submenus (`modules/grid/grid-context-menu-utilities.tsx`). Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R8 (B02-C02).** File: `components/menu/use-menu-touch-hold.ts`. Mechanism: a hold on a row of the open menu dispatches `contextmenu`; `handleContextMenu` calls `event.preventDefault()` before its in-panel return, so `if (!target.dispatchEvent(menu)) dropClickUntil.current = ...` drops the click that ends the press. Trigger: a touch press of 500 ms or more on a row of an open context menu. The row does nothing. Documented intent: "When a handler takes the event, the hook drops the click that ends the hold." Reach: `apps/places/src/components/place-palette/place-palette-panel.tsx` (`PlaceMenu` in context mode). Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R9 (B02-C03).** File: `components/menu/use-menu-pointer.tsx`. Mechanism: corridor progress was measured from a fixed anchor (`(point.x - from.x) / run`), and the anchor moves only on an accepted arrival, so a pointer parked inside the triangle stayed "traveling". Trigger: hover a submenu row, move diagonally toward its panel, stop on a sibling row inside the triangle. The cursor freezes. Documented intent: "which is what keeps one parked mid-corridor from freezing the cursor there." Reach: grid column menu submenus. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R10 (B02-C04).** File: `components/menu/menu-sub.tsx`. Mechanism: `collapse` always calls `triggerRef.current?.focus()` on the submenu's own row, also in a dropdown, where the trigger keeps focus and rows rove by `aria-activedescendant`. Trigger: in a dropdown, open a submenu by keyboard and press Escape or the back arrow in it. Focus lands on the row in the panel, and Tab then leaves the dropdown open. Documented intent: "True for a placement-driven dropdown, where a MenuTrigger keeps focus while open." Reach: `docs/pages/components/menu/submenus.tsx`. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R11 (B02-C06).** File: `components/menu/menu-sub.tsx`. Mechanism: the blur guard tested `rows?.contains(event.relatedTarget)`, the viewport, while the panel around it is focusable (`tabIndex={-1}`) and padded. Trigger: hover a submenu open in a context menu and press its padding, a heading, a separator, or the gap between rows. The submenu closes under the press. Documented intent: "Focus landing *inside* the panel is the open completing, not a rove away". Reach: grid column menu submenus. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R12 (B02-C10).** File: `recipes/kata/menu.ts`. Mechanism: `trigger: mode('data-open:bg-zinc-950/5', 'dark:data-open:bg-white/5')` has no forced-colors pair; in a context or static menu the parent row has lost focus once the pointer works in the panel. Trigger: forced colors, an open submenu with the pointer in its panel. The trail row shows no mark. Documented intent: "so the row the pointer traveled from still reads as the live trail back". Reach: grid column menu submenus. Severity: medium. Narrowed: dropdowns keep `data-active`, which `hannou/active.ts` paints in forced colors. Prior art: NONE.

**R13 (B03-C04).** File: `recipes/kiso/omote/popover.ts`. Mechanism: `export const popover = [blur.md, bg.popover, ring.default]` draws its edge with a `ring-1` box-shadow, which forced colors remove, and the flat PopoverContent and TooltipContent add no outline. Trigger: forced colors with a flat Popover or Tooltip open. The panel has no edge. Documented intent: `sen` owns "forced-colors safety nets; how an element draws its edges". Reach: `apps/places` tooltips (`places-app.tsx`, `place-review-field.tsx`, `trips-index.tsx`, `places-index.tsx`). Severity: medium. Narrowed: the fill keeps its alpha, so text stays readable. Prior art: NONE.

**R14 (B03-C07).** File: `primitives/popover/popover-panel.tsx`. Mechanism: the autofocus layout effect called `selected.focus()` and `panelRef.current.focus()` without `preventScroll`, in the commit that mounts the panel, while Floating UI still holds the wrapper at `left: 0; top: 0` (`computePosition(...).then(...)`). Trigger: open a Listbox or a context menu on a page that the window scrolls. The page jumps to the top. Documented intent: the scroll helper the effect calls scrolls "without affecting any outer scroll containers". Reach: docs site (`docs/pages/components/listbox/with-prefix.tsx`, `in-a-form.tsx`, `modules/chart/legend-placement.tsx`); places never scrolls the window. `useA11yAutoFocus`, which PopoverContent uses, had the same call. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R15 (B01b-C03).** File: `recipes/kata/sheet.ts`. Mechanism: the grip of a sheet is an `absolute z-10` strip of `py-3` (or `px-3`) around a 6 px bar, about 30 px thick. The inset of the slot next to it is `density-p*-[4,5,6,7,8]`, which is 16 px to 32 px. Below `xl` the strip lies over the edge of that slot. Trigger: `<SheetPanel side="top" handle>` with its default footer; a press on the lower 6 px of Close at `md` starts a resize, and the click does not reach the button. The widened test found the same cover on each side: a `left` sheet covers its Close, and a `bottom` sheet covers a control in its title. Documented intent: "A sheet docked across gets a strip on its edge, not a cover over the whole panel." Reach: sheet playground. Severity: medium. Prior art: NONE.

**R16 (B02b-C06).** File: `components/menu/menu-trigger.tsx`. Mechanism: a Space keydown on an open dropdown arms the trigger (`activationHeldRef.current = true`) and roving clicks the active row, which closes the menu. The keyup is cancelled only when the trigger is not armed (`if (!activationHeldRef.current) event.preventDefault()`). An engine that sends the native button click on the keyup after a cancelled keydown reaches `useClick`, which opens the menu again. Trigger: Firefox, a dropdown with a `<button>` trigger, ArrowDown, Space. Documented intent: "Fires `onAction` and closes the menu on click or Enter/Space." Reach: each dropdown with a `<button>` trigger. Severity: medium if Q7 holds. Prior art: NONE.
